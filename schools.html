What is still to confirm

Marked pending, not quietly omitted

School names, programme lists and recruiter lists are placeholders carrying a to-do in the data file until they are confirmed against rvu.edu.in and the placement sheet. They render as [pending] rather than as a plausible guess, because a wrong programme list is worse than a visibly missing one — and because the live RV University placements page currently ships six school links that lead nowhere, which is the specific failure this page exists to avoid.

Per-school distributions are not in the placement sheet yet either. Each school below shows the university-wide spread with a note saying so, because the design does not permit a median without a spread beside it.
