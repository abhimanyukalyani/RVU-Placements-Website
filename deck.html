The mechanism

One source, and empty boxes made impossible

No number is typed into any page. Every figure resolves from one data file through one renderer, which enforces the display rules in code rather than leaving them to whoever edits the page next.

data/placements.js cohort: { total_graduates: NNN, … } index.html <span data-fig="placements.cohort.offers_made"></span> js/render.js fig(null) → "[XXX]" never 0, never a dash pct(n, null) → throws figure row → stamp inserted if absent max sans median → throws checkViews() → throws on a percentage with no denominator in the same view

The empty statistic box on the live page is not styled away here. It is unreachable: there is no code path that renders an unpopulated figure as a blank or a zero.

Eligibility is written throughout as how to stay eligible, never as grounds for disqualification. Same facts, opposite framing — and the same rule governs every error message on the site.

The checker is a pure function, so its whole input space can be enumerated: 1,536 combinations reach 7 branches, and every one carries at least one link and one next action. There is no combination of answers that leaves a student with nothing to do.

No peer site in the audit publishes drive windows at all. The ledger does, with each drive’s CGPA floor beside it.

02 · For recruiters

Sorted by intent, and told what happens next

The single most winnable gap in the brief. Waterloo is the benchmark: classify by stage first, then route. No Indian site in the audit tells a recruiter what happens after they submit.

Router, first thing on the page
  • We want to hire now
  • We are planning to hire later this year
  • We want to offer internships or projects
  • We just want information — never sees a form

The intake gives before it takes. Step three takes no input: it tells the recruiter which schools match, how many students that is, and when the next window closes — computed from the live school and drive records, before anything is submitted.

No pricing. What the university provides at no charge is listed instead: pre-screened shortlists, campus logistics, interview infrastructure, a a single coordinating role that stays with the drive.

What happensWithin
We acknowledge your request1 working day
We send a cohort and eligibility shortlist3 working days
We confirm your drive slot5 working days
The drive runsYour stated window

Published as target service levels, so a recruiter can hold the office to them. Stating them is the differentiator; nobody else in the audit does.

On submit the form logs a structured record rather than posting it anywhere. The intake is built to hand over to the sheet the office already keeps — the column names shipped here are illustrative and are the first thing to reconcile with Corporate Relations.

03 · For parents

Give parents a job, not a photograph

Stanford is the only model in twelve sites, and the instruction from the audit is to copy the move rather than the page. A parent who is given something to do is more useful than a parent who is reassured.

Action one

Refer a recruiter

You know an organisation that hires people with your child’s skills. Tell us and we approach them. Routes into the same intake the recruiters use.

Action two

Offer half an hour

An informational interview with a student in your field. No hiring, no commitment beyond the thirty minutes.

Action three

Join the network

A quarterly briefing, and first sight of drive outcomes as they are recorded rather than months later.

Zero unexplained abbreviations. Lakh per annum, cost to company, cumulative grade point average, pre-placement offer — each expanded at first use and defined in a glossary. Every figure gets a sentence saying what it means.

And an honest section no peer site attempts: what placement does not guarantee. Not every student who wants a placement receives an offer. The figures describe last year’s market. Trust is the deliverable on this page, and hedging would cost it.

The most quotable sentence on the site sits here: “We lead with the median rather than the highest offer because one exceptional offer tells you nothing about your child’s likely outcome.” The page then renders the maximum at 60% of the median’s size, so the layout makes the same argument the sentence does.

Credibility

Voluntary disclosure, including where we fall short

RV University is not a business school and no regulator asks for this. Reporting on the IPRS pattern voluntarily is the cheapest credibility available, and publishing the deviations is what makes the rest believable.

Which schools are excluded, and whyNone in the audit

Eight schools, six counted. The School of Allied and Healthcare Professions has no graduating cohort yet; SCEPS is continuing education for working professionals. Both appear on the site with the reason attached. A school that is silently missing from a placement page is indistinguishable from one that did badly — which is the specific criticism the audit makes of otherwise strong reporting elsewhere.

“No external audit yet; adoption planned” is the honest entry in the deviations table today, and a stronger signal than silence. An empty deviations table would be the least believable thing on the site.

Recorded at three months post-graduation, published at six, raw data retained twelve. Offers outside India converted and adjusted for purchasing power, so one Singapore offer cannot distort a distribution.

What it takes to ship

This design cannot go stale

The audit’s central finding is that peer pages fail because they are unmaintained, not because they are ugly. A redesign that looks better and rots at the same rate has not solved the problem. So the question is not how this looks on launch day — it is what it looks like in eighteen months.

How placement pages rotWhy it cannot happen here
Figures hard-coded into markup, updated by hand once a yearNo number exists in any page. One file, and the whole site moves.
A statistic box empties when a value goes missingA missing value renders [XXX]. There is no code path to a blank.
A percentage outlives the cohort it describedEvery figure carries its record date, inserted by the renderer.
Links to brochures that were moved or deletedZero dead links is an acceptance criterion; 152 checked on every pass.
A new school launches and the page is never updatedSchools come from data, including the two not yet in the cohort.
Porting to rvu.edu.in

A page template, not a rebuild

  • No new webfont. Playfair Display and Cantarell are already enqueued by the current theme.
  • No new colour. The nine tokens mirror the theme’s own --gcid-* custom properties.
  • No framework, no build step. Plain CSS and classic scripts; it opens by double-clicking a file.
  • Figures move to ACF fields or a scheduled sync from the placement sheet. Keeping every number in one place is what makes that a swap rather than a rewrite — and it resolves the no-JavaScript fallback at the same time, because the markup then arrives with the numbers already in it.
  • Semantic, shallow markup that maps to clean blocks in the existing page builder.

The maintenance cost of this page after launch is editing one data file. That is the argument, and it is the reason the design will still be true in a year.
