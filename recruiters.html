Cost

What the university provides at no charge

There is no fee to recruit at RV University. What you get for that:

  • A pre-screened shortlist against the eligibility criteria you set
  • Campus logistics — rooms, scheduling, invigilation for written rounds
  • Interview infrastructure, on campus or virtual, at your preference
  • A named coordinator who stays with your drive from slot to offer

If a cost ever applies to something, it will be stated here before you are asked for anything.

Step by step

How to hire here

  1. Send a hiring request, or write to recruit@rvu.edu.in. Say what stage you are at — hiring now, planning, or internships.
  2. We reply with the schools that match, the cohort size, and the windows that fit your start date.
  3. You confirm the role and the eligibility criteria. We publish it to the students it is open to.
  4. We send the shortlist. You interview on campus or virtually.
  5. Offers go to students through the office, so the one-offer policy is applied consistently and seats are released quickly.
Write a job description that reaches the right cohort

Four things decide whether the right students see your role. State the function in the words a student would use, not an internal title. Give a CGPA floor only if you will actually apply it — every point of it narrows the pool. Say where the work happens and whether that is negotiable. Name the start date, or the earliest one you would accept: it is the single most common reason a strong candidate withdraws late.
