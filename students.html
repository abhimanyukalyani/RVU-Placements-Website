If this is your first visit, read this paragraph and nothing else

You do not need to do anything about placements in your first year except one thing: book the strengths and interests session. It is how the office learns what you want, and everything below follows from it. If you are further along, jump to your year or check where you stand.

Staying eligible

How to stay eligible

Eligibility is a short list of things you keep current, not a list of ways to be excluded. Register once for the cycle and you are in the pool for every drive in it. Keep a current CV and transcript on file and you never miss a window while paperwork catches up.

Each drive sets its own CGPA floor, so your CGPA opens some drives now and more with your next semester’s result. If you have a backlog, clearing it reopens every drive it was holding you out of — the office will put you in touch with your faculty coordinator to plan the attempt.

One offer at a time. Accept an offer and you step out of the pool so the next student gets the seat. Decline it and you are back in the same day, and declining is not recorded against you.
